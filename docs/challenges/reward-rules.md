# Challenge Reward Rules

These are the coin rules for DragonCode challenges (for now).

## Earning coins

- **Correct answer = 10 coins.**
- **Each question can reward coins only once.** Every question has an id (like `stack-1`). The first time a player answers that question correctly, they get 10 coins. If they answer the same question correctly again later, they get 0 coins.

## Wrong answers

- **Incorrect answer = no coins and no penalty.** The player does not lose coins.
- The player can **try again**. The answer they picked is marked wrong so they can't pick it twice, and they can choose from the other answers.
- After a wrong answer, the game shows a **hint**. The hint points the player in the right direction but never gives the answer away.
- A player still earns the full 10 coins if they get the question right after a wrong try.

## After a correct answer

- The game shows a short **explanation** of why the answer is right.
- The player can move on to the next question.

## Quick example

| What happens | Coins earned |
| --- | --- |
| Player answers `stack-1` correctly on the first try | +10 |
| Player answers `stack-2` wrong, reads the hint, then gets it right | +10 |
| Player replays `stack-1` and answers correctly again | +0 (already collected) |

Stack Village has 3 questions, so a player can earn up to **30 coins** there. Since an egg costs 20 coins, 2 correct answers are enough to buy one.
